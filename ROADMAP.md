# Roadmap

Driven by what working designers ask for. Open an issue to vote or propose.

## Next
- **Real-world calibration of the Logo Lab score** against a set of well-crafted and problematic marks
  (anonymised), so the thresholds match designers' judgement.
- **Case studies:** two or three anonymised before/after projects showing what the checks caught.
- **Short video:** install in the Claude app and run the Logo Lab on a real mark.
- Hosted Logo Lab page (GitHub Pages) linked from the README.

## Later
- **Figma plugin** running the Logo Lab checks on the selected frame (the most requested item in early feedback).
- Illustrator script/panel to export clean SVG masters and run the audit.
- Brand book generator (HTML/PDF) from `.brand/identity.json`.
- Shelf-test library of category conventions (no third-party logos shipped).

## Not planned
- Generating final logos without a designer in the loop.
- Trademark clearance (it needs a lawyer and the registers).
