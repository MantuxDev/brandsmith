# Tazzina Nuda — Brand

> Fictional example project for Brandsmith.

## Brief
- **Client / owner:** Tazzina Nuda, specialty coffee roaster, Bologna (fictional)
- **What they do (one sentence):** Roast single-origin coffee and sell it in their shop and online, with full transparency on origin and price paid to farmers.
- **Why now:** Opening online shop and subscription; current hand-made logo doesn't work on packaging or small screens.
- **Scope:** Logo, visual identity, tone of voice, packaging system, basic guidelines.
- **Deliverables & deadline:** Final identity by 2026-12-20.
- **Budget band / constraints:** Medium; two concept rounds.
- **Must keep / must avoid:** Keep the name. Avoid coffee-bean and cup clichés, avoid "Italian tricolour" codes.
- **Decision-makers:** Two founders, joint decision.

## Audience
- **Primary audience:** Urban 25–40, curious about coffee, buys quality food, distrusts marketing fluff.
- **What they need:** To know what they're drinking and why it costs more.
- **Key touchpoints:** Coffee bags (250 g/1 kg), shop sign, Instagram, e-commerce, takeaway cups.

## Market
- **Category:** Specialty coffee roasters, northern Italy.
- **Direct competitors:** 9 audited (see research/competitor-audit.md).
- **Category visual conventions:** Kraft paper, hand-drawn illustration, vintage serif, earthy palettes.
- **White space:** Clean, almost clinical transparency — "nothing to hide" — with warmth from colour, not from craft clichés.

## Positioning
- **For** curious coffee drinkers **who** want to know what's in their cup, **Tazzina Nuda is** the specialty roaster **that** shows everything — origin, roast, price paid — **because** every bag carries the full receipt of its journey. **Unlike** craft roasters who sell a romantic story, **it** sells the facts.

## Personality
- **Traits:** honest but not cold · precise but not technical · warm but not cute · confident but not loud
- **Archetype:** Sage (primary), Everyman (secondary)

## Messaging
- **Promise:** Coffee with nothing to hide.
- **Pillars:** Full transparency · Precise roasting · Fair price for farmers
- **Proof points:** Price paid per kg printed on every bag; roast date and profile; farm named.
