# Publishing Logo Lab on Figma Community

Everything needed for the listing. Publishing needs the author's Figma account (Plugins → Development →
Publish… on the imported plugin). Expect a review by Figma before it goes live.

**Name:** Logo Lab

**Tagline (≤ 100 chars):** Stress-test a logo: smallest safe size, 20+ checks and a client-ready PDF. No AI.

**Description:**
Logo Lab tests the logo you select. It measures the thinnest stroke and the narrowest gap and tells you the
smallest size at which they stay above 1 px, with the reason in plain words. It is a measurement, not a taste score.

Then it runs more than 20 visual checks: a scale ladder from 128 to 16 px, real pixels, blur, negative space,
visual weight, clear space, app-icon keylines, favicon in browser tabs, type lockups, business card, photography,
billboard, pattern, watermark, one colour and reversed, materials, emboss and deboss, halftone, embroidery,
curvature combs and anchor points.

Download a PDF report with your studio's name and a note for the client. The client version shows the minimum
size and the tests without any score.

Runs entirely inside Figma: nothing is uploaded, no account, no AI. Free and open source (MIT).
